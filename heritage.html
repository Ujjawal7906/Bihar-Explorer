Bihar's Historical Timeline

600 BCE

Magadha Empire

One of the earliest large empires of South Asia. Magadha became the cradle of pan-Indian governance and philosophy, laying the groundwork for future civilizations.

321 BCE

Mauryan Empire

Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan Empire from Pataliputra (modern Patna), creating India's first truly pan-continental empire — later elevated by Ashoka's moral vision.

319 CE

Gupta Empire

Bihar's golden age of art, literature, science, and philosophy. Aryabhata, the mathematician-astronomer, lived and worked here during this era of extraordinary intellectual flowering.

427 CE

Nalanda University

The world's first residential university — 10,000 students, 2,000 teachers, a 9-story library. Scholars like Xuanzang and Yijing traveled from China and Southeast Asia to study here.

2025 CE

Modern Bihar

Bihar continues to honor its magnificent heritage while building a modern identity. The state welcomes millions of pilgrims and travelers from over 120 countries annually.

UNESCO Heritage Sites

Mahabodhi Temple Complex
Bodh Gaya, Gaya District

The Mahabodhi Temple stands at the very spot where Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment under the sacred Bodhi Tree around 528 BCE. Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2002, it remains one of the holiest places in Buddhism, drawing pilgrims from over 120 countries every year.

Learn more
Nalanda Mahavihara
Nalanda District, Bihar

The ruins of Nalanda represent the world's first residential university, flourishing from the 5th to 12th century CE. At its zenith it housed 10,000 students and 2,000 teachers from across Asia, teaching theology, logic, grammar, medicine, and mathematics — a monumental feat inscribed in 2016.

Festivals of Bihar

Chhath Puja

Bihar's most iconic festival — four days dedicated to Surya Dev, performed at river ghats at sunrise and sunset with awe-inspiring ritual energy. India's largest open-air festival.

Know more...
Rajgir Mahotsav

A vibrant cultural festival in the ancient hills of Rajgir featuring classical Odissi, Kathak, and folk performances against the stunning backdrop of the Venuvan bamboo grove.

Sonepur Mela

Asia's largest cattle fair on the banks of the Gandak river — a spectacular convergence of animals, artisans, commerce, folk culture, and human diversity spanning 2 weeks.
